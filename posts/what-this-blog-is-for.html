What this blog is for

I will note here when a port has a new release and what it changes for you as a player.

Each note stays short and plain: what the new release gives you, what to do about it, and where to get it. The release itself stays where it was: on the port's release page on GitHub, the same buttons on the ports fetch it.

What you will find here, as I go:

  • New releases and what they change for the player
  • Small playing notes: settings and details worth knowing
  • Nothing else: no roadmaps, no promises about games to come

If something plays differently from the original, tell me in the port's repository on GitHub.
